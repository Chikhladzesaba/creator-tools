Your data stays with you

  • No accounts, no database, no server. What you enter is saved only in your browser, on your device.
  • I can't see your numbers. Nobody can, except you.
  • The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check how it works.
  • I use Google Analytics to count visits to these pages. It never sees what you type into the tools.
  • These are free tools, provided as they are. They help you organise your numbers, but they don't give financial, tax or legal advice.

Good to know: clearing your browser data or switching devices means starting fresh. On a shared computer, use your own browser profile.
